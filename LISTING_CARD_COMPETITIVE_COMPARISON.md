## What Each Section Does

### MATCH (Must Have - Baseline Parity)
```
Component: Top Financial Trio
├─ Asking Price (AED 680K)
│  Purpose: Buyer's investment outlay
│  Psychology: "How much do I need?"
│
├─ Annual Revenue (AED 2.5M)
│  Purpose: Business productivity
│  Psychology: "How much does it make?"
│
└─ Cash Flow Range (AED 500K-2.5M)
   Purpose: Owner's actual take-home
   Psychology: "What's my profit?"

Why Important:
→ These are the ONLY metrics shown by competitors
→ Buyers expect to see them immediately
→ If we don't show them, we look incomplete
→ BUT: Just showing them isn't enough to win
```

### EXCEED (Competitive Moat)

#### Section A: Profitability Intelligence
```
Components: EBITDA, Profit Margin, Growth Rate
├─ EBITDA (AED 750K) - GREEN highlight
│  What it means: Earnings before debt & taxes
│  Why it matters: Standard valuation metric (price/EBITDA multiples)
│  Competitor gap: Most competitors DON'T show this!
│  Buyer benefit: "This is how you value a business"
│
├─ Profit Margin (30%)
│  What it means: EBITDA ÷ Revenue
│  Why it matters: Operational efficiency & health
│  Competitor gap: Never shown
│  Buyer benefit: "Is this business well-run?"
│
└─ YoY Growth (12%) - TRENDING UP
   What it means: Business is accelerating
   Why it matters: Future profitability
   Competitor gap: Not shown
   Buyer benefit: "Will this business be worth MORE next year?"

Forward OS Advantage:
→ Buyer immediately sees if deal is well-run
→ Can compare margins across different businesses
→ Growth rate shows if seller is being truthful about trajectory
```

#### Section B: Buyer Intelligence (ROI + Payback)
```
Components: ROI Projection, Payback Period
├─ ROI Projection (18.5% /yr) - ORANGE highlight
│  Calculation: (Annual Cash Flow / Asking Price) × 100
│  Meaning: "If I buy this, what's my annual return?"
│  Competitor gap: Never calculated for buyer
│  Buyer psychology: THIS IS WHAT THEY CARE ABOUT
│  
│  Example: $100K investment → $18.5K/year return
│  Compares to: Stock market (10%), bonds (4%), savings (0.5%)
│  Message: "This deal beats traditional investments"
│
└─ Payback Period (38 months) - RED highlight
   Calculation: Asking Price ÷ Monthly Cash Flow
   Meaning: "How long until I recoup my investment?"
   Competitor gap: Never shown
   Buyer psychology: Risk metric - shorter is better
   
   What it communicates:
   - 38 months = 3.2 years (reasonable, not risky)
   - Combined with 18.5% ROI = attractive deal
   - If 80+ months = risky (too long)

Forward OS Advantage:
→ Buyers immediately see ROI in business context
→ No need for calculator or spreadsheet
→ Instantly comparable across deals
→ Speaks investor language (not accountant language)
```
