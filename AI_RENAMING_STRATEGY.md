# AI Feature Renaming Strategy

## The Issue
"AI" language can trigger skepticism in business/finance audiences. We want powerful, intelligent features without explicitly calling out the AI component.

## Renaming Guide

| Current Name | New Name | Why | Power Positioning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Valuation** | Valuation Intelligence | Emphasis on insight, not algorithm | "Powered by enterprise-grade valuation intelligence" |
| **AI CIM Generator** | Document Generator / CIM Studio | Practical, focused on output | "Create investor-ready documents in 60 seconds" |
| **AI Diligence Scan** | Due Diligence Analysis | Professional, standard terminology | "8-dimension due diligence assessment" |
| **AI-Powered Insights** | Deal Insights / Strategic Insights | Focus on the output, not method | "Real-time insights on deal momentum" |
| **AI Seriousness Scoring** | Buyer Engagement Scoring / Interest Scoring | Behavior-focused language | "Predict buyer seriousness from viewing patterns" |
| **Predictive Close Probability** | Probability Modeling / Deal Forecast | Statistical, professional | "6-factor close probability analysis" |

## Updated Feature Descriptions

### CIM Studio

### Due Diligence Analysis
> "Comprehensive 8-dimension due diligence assessment identifying risks and opportunities across financials, legal, operations, and market positioning."

### Strategic Heat Maps
> "Real-time market visibility showing deal momentum across industries, geographies, and buyer cohorts."

### Deal Insights
> "Automated analysis of buyer engagement, market context, and deal progression intelligence."

### Buyer Engagement Scoring
> "Predict buyer seriousness from viewing patterns, document requests, and interaction velocity."

## Why This Works

1. **Removes Fear Factor** — "Intelligence" and "Analysis" feel controllable; "AI" feels unpredictable
2. **Emphasizes Outcomes** — Focus on what it DOES, not HOW it does it
3. **Professional Language** — Matches buyer expectations in finance/M&A
4. **Maintains Power** — Still conveys sophistication without the stigma
5. **Future-Proof** — If algorithm improves, no messaging change needed

### Messaging Framework
> **Instead of:** "AI does this"  
> **Say:** "[Feature] shows you [Outcome] through [Capability]"

Example:
- ❌ "AI analyzes your deal heat"
- ✅ "Strategic Heat Maps show real-time market momentum"

## Result
Forward OS remains **equally powerful and intelligent**, but feels **more trustworthy, controllable, and professional** to enterprise decision-makers.
