# Forward OS - Deal Progression System

## Overview

The Deal Progression System tracks all deals through their complete lifecycle from initial interest through closing. Every deal moves through 9 distinct stages, with real-time visibility for sellers, buyers, and brokers.

---

## The 9 Deal Stages

### 1. **INTEREST** 👁️
**Definition:** Initial buyer interest in the deal  
**Timeline:** Days 0-7  
**What Happens:**
- Buyer discovers deal on marketplace
- Buyer saves/bookmarks the deal
- Buyer may send initial inquiry
- Seller receives interest notification

**Seller Actions:**
- Monitor who's interested
- Prepare preliminary information packet
- Update deal listing if needed

**Buyer Actions:**
- Review deal overview
- Ask initial questions
- Decide whether to pursue

**Exit Condition:** Buyer requests data room access

---

### 2. **QUALIFICATION** ✓
**Definition:** Buyer verified, KYC complete  
**Timeline:** Days 7-14  
**What Happens:**
- Buyer completes KYC verification
- Buyer's financial credentials verified
- Buyer signs NDA
- Seller evaluates buyer seriousness
- Initial data room access granted

**Seller Actions:**
- Verify buyer is legitimate
- Assess buyer financial capability
- Grant limited data room access (Stage 1 documents)

**Buyer Actions:**
- Complete all KYC requirements
- Sign NDA agreement
- Review Stage 1 documents (overview, highlights)

**Exit Condition:** Buyer requests more detailed financial information

---

### 3. **DUE DILIGENCE** 📋
**Definition:** Detailed financial & operational review  
**Timeline:** Days 14-35  
**What Happens:**
- Buyer accesses full data room (Stage 2 & 3)
- Buyer reviews all financials, tax returns, customer contracts
- Buyer may request additional information
- Seller responds to information requests
- Buyer conducts site visits/calls with management

**Seller Actions:**
- Provide all requested documents
- Facilitate buyer calls with management
- Answer detailed questions about operations
- Track buyer engagement via analytics

**Buyer Actions:**
- Deep dive into financials (3-5 year history)
- Review customer concentration, retention
- Assess operational processes
- Request additional docs if needed

**Engagement Metrics Tracked:**
- Pages viewed: 15-25 pages
- Time spent: 200+ minutes
- Documents downloaded: 10-20 docs
- Questions asked: 5-15 questions

**Exit Condition:** Buyer is satisfied with DD and wants to make offer

---

### 4. **LOI** 📝
**Definition:** Letter of Intent signed (pre-offer)  
**Timeline:** Days 35-42  
**What Happens:**
- Buyer and seller sign LOI (non-binding)
- LOI outlines key terms (valuation range, timeline, exclusivity)
- Exclusivity period starts (90-120 days)
- Formal binding negotiation begins

**Seller Actions:**
- Negotiate LOI terms
- Agree to exclusivity period
- Provide additional legal documents as needed

**Buyer Actions:**
- Prepare LOI with proposed terms
- Negotiate key provisions
- Commit to further due diligence

**Key LOI Terms:**
- Valuation range (min-max)
- Proposed closing date
- Exclusivity period
- Financing contingencies
- Key conditions to close

**Exit Condition:** Both parties sign LOI

---

### 5. **OFFERS** 💰
**Definition:** Multiple formal offers received  
**Timeline:** Days 42-56  
**What Happens:**
- Buyer(s) submit formal purchase offers
- Multiple offers may be received (creates competition)
- Seller reviews and compares offers
- Initial negotiations on key terms begin
- Heat maps show high activity

**Seller Actions:**
- Collect formal offers with complete terms
- Compare offers (valuation, terms, timing, buyer strength)
- Begin negotiating with top candidates
- Keep losing bidders informed

**Buyer Actions:**
- Submit detailed purchase offer
- Include all key terms and contingencies
- Demonstrate financing capability
- Respond to seller questions

**Offer Components:**
- Purchase price (enterprise value)
- Working capital adjustments
- Seller financing (if any)
- Earnout structure
- Contingencies (financing, DD, regulatory)
- Timeline to close

**Exit Condition:** Seller selects preferred buyer and begins detailed negotiation

---

### 6. **NEGOTIATION** 🤝
**Definition:** Detailed term negotiation with preferred buyer  
**Timeline:** Days 56-100  
**What Happens:**
- Intensive negotiations on all purchase agreement terms
- Back-and-forth on:
  - Purchase price (final)
  - Reps & warranties
  - Indemnification
  - Escrow arrangements
  - Closing conditions
- Legal teams draft and revise agreement
- Management presentations to buyer's team

**Seller Actions:**
- Negotiate final terms with buyer
- Provide full disclosure documents
- Facilitate management calls & meetings
- Ensure legal protections

**Buyer Actions:**
- Conduct final due diligence
- Negotiate purchase agreement
- Prepare for financing (if needed)
- Final management presentations

**Key Negotiations:**
- Final valuation
- Payment structure (cash, stock, earnout)
- Working capital treatment
- Tax representations
- Seller indemnification period/amount
- Earnout terms (if applicable)

**Exit Condition:** Both parties agree on all material terms

---

### 7. **FINAL AGREEMENT** ✅
**Definition:** Purchase Agreement signed (binding)  
**Timeline:** Days 100-110  
**What Happens:**
- Definitive purchase agreement signed
- Financing confirmed (if required)
- Final closing preparations begin
- Regulatory approvals obtained (if needed)
- Employee notifications prepared

**Seller Actions:**
- Execute definitive purchase agreement
- Ensure financing is confirmed
- Begin employee transition planning
- Prepare closing documents

**Buyer Actions:**
- Finalize financing
- Obtain regulatory approvals
- Prepare integration plan
- Notify key stakeholders

**Final Steps:**
- Sign purchase agreement
- Confirm financing/cash
- Regulatory clearances
- Obtain third-party consents
- Prepare employee communications
- Schedule closing date

**Exit Condition:** All closing conditions satisfied

---

### 8. **CLOSING** 🏁
**Definition:** Final documentation and funds transfer  
**Timeline:** Days 110-115  
**What Happens:**
- Final closing meeting
- Seller's counsel reviews closing docs
- Buyer's counsel reviews closing docs
- Funds transferred to seller
- Company documents transferred to buyer
- Regulatory filings completed

**Seller Actions:**
- Sign closing documents
- Receive final purchase price
- Facilitate employee transition
- Provide final training/handoff

**Buyer Actions:**
- Wire funds to seller
- Take possession of all documents
- Complete financing documentation
- Begin operations transition

**Closing Deliverables:**
- Executed stock/asset purchase agreement
- Seller's closing certificate
- Shareholder resolutions
- Good standing certificates
- Transfer of ownership documents
- Regulatory filings
- Employee agreements
- Customer notifications

**Exit Condition:** Funds transferred and documents exchanged

---

### 9. **CLOSED** 🎉
**Definition:** Deal completed - ownership transferred  
**Timeline:** Day 115+  
**What Happens:**
- Ownership fully transferred
- Buyer controls operations
- Earn-outs managed (if applicable)
- Any seller obligations completed
- Deal marked as successful

**Post-Close Activities:**
- Monitor earnout conditions (if applicable)
- Seller training/transition continues (30-90 days)
- Integration with buyer's operations
- Customer relationship transition
- Employee retention management

## Critical Alerts

**Yellow Flags:**
- Deal stuck in DD for >28 days (typical is 21)
- Deal stalled in Negotiation for >60 days
- Buyer engagement drops (fewer page views, less time)

**Red Flags:**
- No activity for >7 days
- Buyer stops responding to seller
- Engagement scores dropping
- Deal hasn't progressed in 45 days

---

## Best Practices

**For Sellers:**
1. Move deals quickly through Interest & Qualification
2. Ensure full engagement during Due Diligence
3. Use engagement analytics to identify serious buyers
4. Don't leave deals in Offers stage >14 days (creates uncertainty)
5. Track all communications in system

**For Buyers:**
1. Complete DD thoroughly (don't skip items)
2. Respond quickly to seller questions
3. Keep engagement high (shows seriousness)
4. Ask all questions upfront (don't surprise seller)

**For Brokers:**
1. Monitor timeline - deals shouldn't stall
2. Help negotiate when needed
3. Keep both sides moving forward
4. Flag risks early
