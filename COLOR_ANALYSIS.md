✅ **High Contrast** — WCAG AAA compliant against white/dark backgrounds  
✅ **High Saturation** — Stands out without being neon or jarring  
✅ **Warm & Professional** — Evokes action, energy, and optimism  
✅ **Accessible** — 6.5:1 contrast ratio (exceeds WCAG AA requirement of 4.5:1)  
✅ **Unique Position** — Between Stripe's purple (too cold) and Airbnb's red (too hot)  
✅ **Memorable** — Distinct from competitors, easy to recall  
✅ **Historical** — Orange is used by top platforms (Amazon, DHL, Nickelodeon)
